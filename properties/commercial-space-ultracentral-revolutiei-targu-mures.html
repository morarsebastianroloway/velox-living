Commercial Space in the Centre of Târgu Mureș

This 90 m² commercial space is located on Strada Revoluției in the ultracentral area of Târgu Mureș — one of the most visible and high-footfall streets in the city. With a prominent storefront and two separate entrances (from the street and through the courtyard), the space is ideally suited for retail, a showroom, a medical or beauty practice, or a professional office.

The layout is practical and versatile, with a separate bathroom included. The space benefits from central heating, air conditioning, thermopane windows, and an alarm system with intercom — ensuring comfort and security year-round.

Full connectivity infrastructure is already in place: high-speed internet, an internal computer network (structured cabling), video monitoring, and POS payment terminal infrastructure — making it ready for immediate business use with minimal setup.

Building control panel access is available on the upper floor. Parking is readily available on three adjacent streets: Strada Avram Iancu, Aleea Scăricica, and Strada Revoluției itself.

The asking price is €1,500/month, negotiable.
