Fully Renovated Modern House — Move-In Ready

A spacious, bright and fully renovated modern family house with 175 m² of usable space (213 m² built). It sits in a very quiet area on a private street, at the edge of Târgu Mureș towards Sângeorgiu de Mureș, near Strada Nordului / 7 Noiembrie. The house comes fully finished and fully furnished — interior, exterior and garden — so you can move in straight away.

The ground floor has a spacious living and dining area (42 m²), a separate kitchen (14 m²), an office (14 m²) that can also be used as a ground-floor bedroom, a pantry and storage closet, a bathroom, and a 16 m² terrace.

Upstairs there are three bedrooms (18 m², 15 m² and 14 m²), two walk-in dressing rooms (3–4 m² each), a bathroom and a balcony.

The plot measures 450 m², plus a 50 m² share of the private access road. The yard is fully landscaped: premium Semmelrock paving (Appia Antica), lawn and ornamental trees, programmable smart exterior lighting, a front fence with concrete base and posts and metal mesh, a 6-metre automated Nice gate with separate access for two cars, intercom and a smart module for remote control, parking for two cars, and a wooden tool shed.

Technical features and comfort: photovoltaic panels with a 15 kW battery, gas central heating, air conditioning in the living room and one of the bedrooms, and large windows with generous glazing for excellent natural light. All utilities are connected — water, gas, electricity and sewerage — and the asphalt road is just 100 m away.

Excellent location: County Hospital 2 km, Târgu Mureș city centre 3.5 km, Lidl 800 m, Medex Hospital 1.3 km, walking access to the Weekend leisure area, and public transport only 700 m away. Rent is €990/month. The house is also available for sale at €349,900.
