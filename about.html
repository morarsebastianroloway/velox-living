Our Story

Rooted in Târgu Mureș. Built for Owners Everywhere.

Velox Living grew out of a simple frustration: property owners living abroad — in Western Europe, Latin America, or beyond — had no reliable, communicative partner to trust with their Transylvanian investments. The options were either informal arrangements with limited accountability, or large agencies that treated every property like a number.

We started the way most landlords do — managing our own apartments in Târgu Mureș. That experience taught us what tenants actually need, what breaks, what causes disputes, and what matters most to owners at the end of every month. We didn't learn property management from a textbook; we lived it first as landlords ourselves.

So we built something different. As a family-run team with deep roots in Mureș County, we combine the responsiveness of a personal service with the professionalism of a dedicated management firm. Every property we manage receives the same care we give our own — because we still have skin in the game.

We work exclusively in Transylvania — which means we know the local market, the contractors, the regulations, and the neighbourhoods intimately. When you hand us the keys, you gain a trusted partner on the ground, not just a management contract.

Multilingual Service

We Speak Your Language — Literally.

Property management in Transylvania sits at a unique cultural crossroads. Târgu Mureș is a bilingual city where both Romanian and Hungarian are everyday languages — and many of our clients are international investors who work in English or Spanish.

That's why Velox Living operates fluently in all four languages — not just translating words, but navigating local customs, legal terminology, and tenant expectations in each language natively.

English Full service — consultations, contracts, reports & support
Română Native — all ANAF filings, contracts & local administration
Magyar Native — tenant communication, negotiations & local dealings
Español Full service — for clients across Spain and Latin America

Why This Matters for Remote Owners

When your tenant calls about a heating failure on a winter evening, you need someone who can respond in Romanian and coordinate the repair — not one or the other. When you receive a contract to review, it should be in the language you're most comfortable in, not the one that's easiest for us.

Our multilingual capability isn't a marketing line — it's how we actually work, every day. Owners receive reports and messages in their preferred language. Tenants are spoken to in theirs. And all official paperwork is handled correctly in Romanian, as Romanian law requires.

Registrations & Compliance

We manage properties in full compliance with Romanian property law — so your investment is protected and every document holds up legally.
