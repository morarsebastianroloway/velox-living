Why Târgu Mureș Is an Interesting Investment Market

Târgu Mureș (Marosvásárhely in Hungarian, Neumarkt am Mieresch in German) is the capital of Mureș County, with a population of approximately 120,000–130,000 people. It is the largest city in Transylvania after Cluj-Napoca and Brașov, and one of the few genuinely bilingual cities in Romania where both Romanian and Hungarian are spoken as everyday languages.

Demand Drivers

The city's rental market is driven by several structural demand factors that provide a stable foundation for buy-to-let investment:

  • George Emil Palade University (UMFST) — one of Romania's leading medical and pharmacy universities, with approximately 10,000 students, a significant portion of whom rent privately. Medical students tend to be reliable, long-term tenants who stay for 6-year programmes.
  • Healthcare sector professionals — Târgu Mureș hosts multiple hospitals and medical research institutions. Medical professionals represent a high-quality tenant demographic with stable income.
  • Industrial employment base — Manufacturing companies including Romgaz (Romania's state gas producer) and numerous international firms in the local industrial zone provide stable working-class demand for affordable rentals.
  • Bilingual appeal — The city's unique Romanian-Hungarian character attracts tenants from both linguistic communities, broadening the effective tenant pool.

Infrastructure and Connectivity

Târgu Mureș has a functioning international airport (TGM) with seasonal and regular connections to major European hubs. The road network connecting it to Cluj-Napoca (approximately 110 km) and Brașov (approximately 150 km) has seen continued investment. For investors, this means the city is accessible for periodic visits and has genuine long-term growth potential rather than isolation risk.

Price Environment in 2026

Average apartment acquisition prices in Târgu Mureș in 2026 range approximately from €800–€1,400 per square metre for existing stock, depending on location, floor, and condition. New-build properties in premium developments reach €1,500–€1,900/m². Central and western residential neighbourhoods (Tudor, Unirii, Cornești) command the highest rents relative to purchase price. These figures represent significant discounts — typically 40%–60% — versus equivalent properties in Bucharest or Cluj-Napoca.

Average Rental Yields in Târgu Mureș

Gross rental yields in Târgu Mureș typically range from 5% to 8% per year, depending on the property type, neighbourhood, condition, and the quality of tenants secured. This compares favourably with major Western European markets and is competitive with the larger Romanian cities when accounting for lower acquisition costs.

A Practical Yield Example

A 2-bedroom apartment in good condition in the Tudor neighbourhood, purchased for €80,000 and rented at €420/month, generates gross annual income of €5,040 — a gross yield of 6.3%. After management fees (approximately 10%), building association fees, and the 10% Romanian income tax (applied to 60% of gross income under the standard deduction), net yield is approximately 4.5%–5.2%, depending on vacancy and maintenance costs.

Yield by Property Type

  • Studio / 1-bedroom apartments (garsonieră/apartament 1 cameră): Strong demand from students; yields 6%–8%; lower absolute rent but very low vacancy.
  • 2-bedroom apartments: The most liquid segment; yields 5.5%–7%; suits couples, young professionals, and medical residents.
  • 3-bedroom apartments: Yields 5%–6.5%; attracts families and shared professional tenants; slightly longer void periods between tenancies.
  • Commercial / retail spaces: Yields can exceed 8%; higher risk profile; specialist market knowledge essential.

How Transylvania's Rental Market Differs from Bucharest

Investors familiar with Bucharest often find Transylvania's rental market operates on different dynamics. Understanding these differences prevents incorrect assumptions about vacancy rates, tenant behaviour, and management complexity.

The key distinction is that Târgu Mureș is a long-term rental market. The student and professional tenant base typically stays for one to several years. This means lower turnover costs, more stable cash flow, and a simpler management operation compared to the more transactional Bucharest market. For investors seeking passive income rather than capital gains speculation, this stability is a significant advantage.
