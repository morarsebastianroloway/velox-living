Legal & Ownership
Can a foreigner own and rent out property in Romania?

Yes — EU citizens have had the same property ownership rights as Romanian nationals since 2007, and non-EU nationals can own apartments and commercial spaces without restriction. Renting out your property is perfectly legal: you register the rental contract with the local tax authority (ANAF), declare your rental income, and pay a flat income tax. Velox Living handles all the local administrative steps on your behalf so you never need to visit a government office in person.

What are the legal requirements for renting an apartment in Târgu Mureș?

In Târgu Mureș, as across Romania, a legally valid rental requires a written lease contract signed by both parties, registration of that contract with ANAF within 30 days, and the declaration of rental income for tax purposes. The property must also hold a valid energy performance certificate (certificat energetic) before it can be advertised. Our team prepares all contracts in compliance with Romanian civil law and handles ANAF registration for you.

Do I need to be present in Romania to sign contracts with tenants?

No — as a Velox Living client, you do not need to travel to Romania to sign tenant contracts. We sign a property management contract together, through which Velox Living officially represents you as the owner — and it is this contract that allows us to conclude the lease agreement with the tenant on your behalf. All documents are signed digitally, so geography is never an obstacle to managing your investment.

What happens if a tenant doesn't pay rent in Romania?

Romanian law provides landlords with a clear process: if a tenant misses rent payments, the landlord can issue a formal notice and, if unresolved, initiate eviction proceedings through the courts. In practice, we act before disputes escalate — proactive communication, clear lease terms, and security deposit clauses protect our owners. Velox Living monitors payments every month and contacts tenants immediately if a deadline is missed, so problems are resolved early.

How are utility bills handled for rental properties in Romania?

In Romania, utility contracts (electricity, gas, water, building association fees) can be held in either the landlord's or tenant's name — the arrangement is specified in the lease. For owners living abroad, Velox Living can hold the utility accounts and pay bills on your behalf, recovering costs from the tenant each month. This keeps accounts organised, avoids late-payment penalties, and ensures nothing slips through the cracks while you're overseas.
